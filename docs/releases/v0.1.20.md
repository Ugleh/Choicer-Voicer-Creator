## Changes

- Fix unused space beside the preview. Video now fills the available preview area and remains centered. Show lines sits underneath the video resolution.
- Pin the preview and playback controls to keep them visible while scrolling. The pin preference is remembered on this computer.
- Empty Sound Effects sections show only a compact import/generate bar, with no empty timeline lane. Once effects exist, the editor can also be collapsed.
- Add Save As and Ctrl+Shift+S. After saving a new file, Ctrl+S saves to that file. Media stays in its existing locations.
- Right-click a sidebar scene to delete it or exclude it from collection export. Excluded scenes remain editable and can be exported individually. Undo restores deletions and inclusion changes; projects remember exclusions. Sharing statistics include only enabled scenes.
